Beginner tips

Good RDO income habits

  • Keep Moonshine and Trader production moving before starting long active routes.
  • Use Collector or regular bounties as active filler while passive timers run.
  • Prioritize Bounty Hunter when gold is the main goal.
  • Pick local Trader delivery when solo, low rank or avoiding PvP risk.
  • Use monthly bonuses only when they are manually verified for the current month.

Assumptions

Advanced notes and data policy

  • Estimated payout values are planning estimates and should be updated when verified data is available.
  • The planner does not call a live Rockstar or news API. Monthly bonuses are manual data.
  • Cooldowns are client-side timers only and are not linked to your game account.
  • Score weights are intentionally simple so the recommendation logic stays readable.
