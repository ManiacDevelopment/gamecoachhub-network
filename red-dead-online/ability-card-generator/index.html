How this tool scores builds

The generator uses a transparent guide model: cards have tags and directional effects, then weapon type, playstyle, focus and stance adjust the final ratings. It is designed for planning, not for claiming official hidden numbers.

  • Damage and survivability are separated for sniper and general combat.
  • Sustain, mobility and PvP/PvE fit are weighted by your focus.
  • Recommendations flag mismatch between cards, weapons and playstyle.
