Route priority

Grounded rewards preparation. Move from food and water into safe height, measured combat, lab unlocks and boss-specific prep.

  • First 30 minutes: water, food, fiber, sprigs, pebbles and a safe lean-to.
  • Early base: raised, close to water and not directly in insect patrol paths.
  • Progression: build gear for the next lab instead of wandering into high-risk zones.

Opening route

Survive your first day before chasing the whole yard.

The first objective is not damage. It is a repeatable loop: collect safe materials, secure clean water, craft basic tools, mark useful resources and avoid fights that drain healing.

  1. Collect plant fiber, sprigs, pebblets, sap and dry grass before pushing into risky areas.
  2. Use dew, juice drops and safe food sources before combat routes become your main focus.
  3. Place the first lean-to and storage near a route you can recognize at night.
  4. Fight small threats for practice, but do not force Wolf Spider encounters early.

Strategy route

Lab progression should feel like a route, not a wiki list.

Each lab section is prepared around recommended gear, local dangers, boss or mini-boss preparation and the unlocks that meaningfully change your next decision.

  • Scout the route before carrying rare materials.
  • Upgrade gear for the next hazard instead of every item you own.
  • Use markers for resources, labs and safe return paths.
  • Split boss preparation into healing, weapon role, armor role and mutation role.

Tips and habits

Grounded tips worth teaching early.

  • Build the first base raised from the ground so small insects do not reach it easily.
  • Place markers at important resource spots and safe return routes.
  • Use enemy weaknesses intentionally instead of only chasing the highest raw damage.
  • Keep multiple armor sets for different jobs once resources allow it.
  • Do not challenge Wolf Spiders too early.
  • Build a zipline network later to reduce risky travel time.
  • Save rare materials until your build direction is clearer.
  • In multiplayer, let one player focus on base/crafting while another scouts and handles combat.
